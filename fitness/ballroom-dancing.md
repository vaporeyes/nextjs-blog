# Ballroom Dancing

I started ballroom dancing with my wife last year in September and since then we've been dancing an average of a few times a week. We started with the Waltz, Tango, and Foxtrot for smooth dances and the Cha Cha, Rumba and Swing for the rhythm dances.

## Competing

We competed in our first competition in July 2025, which was a local competition in the Middle Tennessee area. We did well, placing 1st in each of our categories: Waltz, Tango, Foxtrot, Cha Cha, Rumba, and Swing, for amateur couples.

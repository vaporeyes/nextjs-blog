Design Concept

  • Dark circle background: Represents the depth and mystery of philosophical inquiry
  • Blue curved path: Symbolizes one modality or way of being
  • Green curved path: Represents another modality, interlocking with the first
  • Golden center point: The inevitable convergence point - the "ineluctable" aspect
  • Overall form: Abstract infinity-like shape suggesting continuous flow and interconnection
